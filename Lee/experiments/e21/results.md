# Lee/e21 diagnostic results

Measured-air replay of the fixed e18 stochastic model yields:

| Reset interval (steps) | NH4 MAE | Predicted NH4 violation (%) | Predicted DO violation (%) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 0.3473 | 15.60 | 0.84 |
| 4 | 0.7272 | 13.25 | 1.71 |
| 16 | 1.3892 | 7.73 | 1.75 |
| 48 | 1.6302 | 7.04 | 1.77 |
| 95 | 1.7509 | 5.64 | 1.77 |

Observed NH4 and DO frequencies are 17.32 and 1.16 percent. The 95-step case
reproduces e18 exactly at checked tolerance. Short forecasts are much more
faithful to observed NH4 frequency; substantial drift remains when forecasts
are chained. This separates an observed error pattern, not its unique cause.

Frequent resets supply observed later states. These diagnostic rows cannot
be interpreted as control policies or as a new closed-loop environment in
which actions cause those observed reset states. The official horizon is not
shortened to manufacture an improvement.

## Logged action support

A training-only 5/95-percent quantile model, adjusted on the 30 inner dates,
has 85.32 percent empirical coverage on development recorded air. The median
band width is 1081.44 m3/h. Scaling recorded air to .75 or 1.25, with the
actual action bound, puts respectively 94.71 and 69.32 percent of probes
outside the estimated band at their original observed states.

These are estimated behavior bands, not proof of zero probability or lack
of every form of overlap. They nevertheless show that global action bounds
alone do not make substantial air interventions well supported by the log.
This check does not characterize states generated by a learned controller,
and factual forecast fidelity does not independently validate counterfactual
quality responses. More observed-state resets do not resolve that issue.

## Provenance and interpretation

Execution commit 3ec262e. Source inputs, dates and residual bank checksum
are checked in diagnostic.py. No fitting to development observations occurs
and no test observation is evaluated. Runtime follows e18. Results and full
forecast metrics are in summary.json.

```powershell
python Threemool/Lee/experiments/e21/diagnostic.py --data kaggle_data/darrow_15min.pkl --innovation-summary results/e18_innovations/summary.json --residual-bank results/e18_innovations/residual_bank.npz --contractive-summary results/e17_contractive/summary.json --out Threemool/Lee/experiments/e21/summary.json
```

These diagnostics motivate multi-step, history-conditioned latent modeling
in e22. They do not establish that an RNN, a mechanistic model or any other
future model must fail, nor that the dataset has no research value.
