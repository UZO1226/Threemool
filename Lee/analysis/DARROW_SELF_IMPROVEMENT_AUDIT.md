The preceding evidence supports a research comparison in a frozen surrogate.
It does not establish real-plant safety, validated action effects, measured
electricity savings, discharge-law compliance, or repaired DO onset forecasts.
Cost is the count of NH4 plus DO violation indicators, not financial expense.
Initial gains are conditional on matched policies and a reused40-day validation
interval. Fresh seeds must pass separately; pooling cannot rescue a failure.

verify_e71_blocks.py independently recomputes every recorded episode aggregate,
asserts exactly the40 validation dates times5 members times8 draws per policy,
checks per-episode cost against the two violation percentages, reconstructs
the complete original air-only matcher from saved query episodes, checks all
independent replay certificates, and recomputes each block's means, sample SD
and paired-seed intervals. Its pending output is deliberately not a success.
Final completion also requires remote artifact verification, correct current
documentation and the full objective, not only a green arithmetic verifier.
